Which engine do I use?

Find out which decision engine powers each i6 Decision Suite product.

1 min read

If you arrived knowing a product's commercial name, use the table below to find the decision engine you need to call.

Product, family and engine

Product Decision family Engine
Relevance Recommendation, Personalization, Propension & Assortment i6RecSys
Forecasting Forecasting, Demand Modeling & Sales Planning i6Previsio
Assortment Recommendation, Personalization, Propension & Assortment i6RecSys + i6Previsio
Sales Planning Forecasting, Demand Modeling & Sales Planning i6Previsio
Pricing Pricing & Elasticity Modeling i6ElasticPrice
Targeting Recommendation, Personalization, Propension & Assortment i6RecSys

The three families

  • Recommendation, Personalization, Propension & Assortment — rankings, carousels, propensity and mix composition.
  • Forecasting, Demand Modeling & Sales Planning — granular demand forecasting and sales targets.
  • Pricing & Elasticity Modeling — elasticity curves and recommended prices.
